Threshold Note

Classification methodology

This model uses a 35% classification threshold instead of the standard 50%. In cardiac screening, missing a sick patient (False Negative) carries higher clinical cost than a false alarm. Lowering the threshold increases Recall from 89.2% → 91.2%.
