Terms of Use & Clinical Disclaimer

CardioRisk Clinical Assessment System is an academic portfolio project built to demonstrate applied machine learning on clinical data. It is NOT a licensed medical device and must not be used to make or influence real patient care decisions.

The underlying Random Forest model was trained on the publicly available UCI Heart Disease Dataset (n=918) and has not undergone clinical validation, regulatory review, or peer-reviewed audit. Dashboard statistics marked "Demo Data" are synthetic and for visualisation purposes only.

By proceeding, you confirm you understand these limitations and will not rely on this tool for any clinical decision whatsoever.
